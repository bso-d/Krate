# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Krate welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Everyone
participating in the project deserves a respectful, inclusive environment free
from harassment and discrimination.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback constructive and focused on the work. Technical criticism and
  good-faith disagreement are welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Respect others' boundaries, privacy, and time.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and correct harmful behavior when asked.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, threats, intimidation,
personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not publish
someone's private information without consent. Retaliation against anyone who
reports a concern or participates in an investigation is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to Krate issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other project spaces, as well as public activity where someone officially
represents the project.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns to the repository maintainers. Start with the
[repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bso-d) and use a private
contact method if one is available. If no private method is listed, open an
issue asking for a private reporting channel **without including incident
details, names, or personal information**. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Once a private channel is established, include relevant links, dates, and a
brief account of what happened. If a maintainer is involved in the concern,
contact another uninvolved maintainer where possible. Maintainers should handle
reports promptly and fairly, limit disclosure to what is necessary to review
and address the incident, and avoid promising absolute confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation. They
should consider the severity, impact, and repetition of the conduct, explain
decisions to those affected where appropriate, and use proportionate measures:

1. A correction or written warning for minor misconduct.
2. A temporary restriction for serious or repeated misconduct.
3. A permanent ban for sustained harassment or severe violations.

Threats, retaliation, or other serious misconduct may warrant immediate removal
or a ban without a prior warning. Participation does not grant a right to abuse
others, and ignoring a warning can result in stronger restrictions.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through
the same reporting channel and provide additional context. An uninvolved
maintainer should review the request where possible.

## Attribution

This policy draws on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
with reporting and enforcement guidance adapted for Krate.
